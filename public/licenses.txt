cities and time zones comes from GeoNames. Their licenses follow.

2. GeoNames
   https://www.geonames.org/

Cities with a population over 15,000 and capitals (cities15000), country
and region names (countryInfo, admin1CodesASCII), their names in the site
languages — Russian, English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Chinese
(alternateNamesV2) and IANA time zones. Licensed under Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: only the name (in the file's language and in Latin), country,
region, time zone and population (rounded to thousands) are kept;
Europe/Kyiv is written as Europe/Kiev for older browsers. The files (one per
language) are built by scripts/build-hd-cities.py
(https://github.com/dvvolkovv/land_linkeon).
